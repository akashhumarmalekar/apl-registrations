Sync site from Excel template

Your standing import template. Choose the sheet and the whole site is updated to match it: players are added or updated, players who are not in the sheet are removed, and the Registered, Pending, Followup, Category and Teams views all follow. Columns: Full Name, Followup, Mobile, Category, Status, Team (Reference is optional; the first row must be the header). You see a preview of every change before anything is applied.

"Not Registered" in the Category or Team column just means blank, and a Registered player with no team shows as Reserved. Columns that are missing from the sheet are left as they are on the site. If the file has several sheets you can pick which one to use in the preview.

Bulk import

To add one person, use the form in the Data tab. To load many at once, load an Excel file below or paste rows copied straight from Excel. Columns: Full Name, Followup, Mobile, Status, Category, Team, Reference (Team and Reference are optional; tab or comma separated). If the first row is a header, columns are matched by their names (Team or Teams both work); without a header they are read in the order above.

Update existing participants matches each row to a player already in the app (by name, or by mobile if the name is not found) and changes only the columns in your file. Everything else (category, reference, status when there is no Status column) stays untouched. The first row must be a header. Names that are not found are listed, not added.
